# GST - interest on delayed payment (sample summary, verify with the Act and notifications)

Section 50(1) of the CGST Act, 2017: a person liable to pay tax who fails to pay it within the prescribed period shall pay interest at the notified rate, currently 18% per annum, for the period of delay.

Section 50(3): where input tax credit has been wrongly availed and utilised, interest is payable at the notified rate (currently 18% per annum, as amended with retrospective effect from 1 July 2017).

Interest is calculated from the day following the due date up to the date of payment. Interest under section 50 is payable on the tax paid by debiting the electronic cash ledger where the return is filed late, as per the proviso to section 50(1).

Show cause notices for tax not paid or short paid: section 73 (cases other than fraud) and section 74 (fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression) for periods up to FY 2023-24; section 74A for FY 2024-25 onwards. Verify the applicable section and time limits for the period involved.
