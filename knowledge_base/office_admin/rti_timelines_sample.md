# Right to Information Act, 2005 - key timelines (sample summary, verify with the Act)

Section 7(1): The Public Information Officer (PIO) shall provide information or reject the request within 30 days of receipt of the request. Where the information concerns the life or liberty of a person, it shall be provided within 48 hours.

Section 6(3): Where the application relates to information held by another public authority, it shall be transferred to that authority within 5 days and the applicant informed.

Section 7(2): If the PIO fails to give a decision within the period, the PIO is deemed to have refused the request.

Section 19(1): First appeal lies to the officer senior in rank to the PIO (First Appellate Authority) within 30 days of the expiry of the period or receipt of the decision. The FAA may admit it after 30 days if sufficient cause is shown.

Section 19(6): The first appeal shall be disposed of within 30 days of receipt, extendable to a total of 45 days with reasons recorded in writing.

Section 8(1)(j): Personal information which has no relationship to any public activity or interest, or which would cause unwarranted invasion of privacy, is exempt unless larger public interest justifies disclosure. Information about a third party's tax returns and assessments is generally treated as personal information; follow the procedure for third-party information under Section 11 where relevant.

Every reply rejecting a request must state the reasons, the period within which an appeal may be filed and the particulars of the appellate authority (Section 7(8)).
