# Income-tax Act, 2025 - transition note (sample, verify with the official text)

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 with effect from 1 April 2026. It uses the concept of "tax year" in place of "previous year" and "assessment year".

Income earned up to 31 March 2026 (FY 2025-26, AY 2026-27) and proceedings for earlier years continue to be governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961, subject to the savings and repeal provisions of the 2025 Act.

When drafting a notice or order, first identify the year involved and cite the Act and section that apply to that year. Section numbers in the 2025 Act are different from the 1961 Act; use the official correlation table issued by the Department.

Budget 2026 kept income-tax slabs unchanged for FY 2026-27. Under the new regime for FY 2025-26: up to Rs 4 lakh nil; 4-8 lakh 5%; 8-12 lakh 10%; 12-16 lakh 15%; 16-20 lakh 20%; 20-24 lakh 25%; above 24 lakh 30%. Rebate makes tax nil for resident individuals with total income up to Rs 12 lakh (normal-rate income). Standard deduction for salaried individuals is Rs 75,000 under the new regime and Rs 50,000 under the old regime. Health and Education Cess is 4%.

Interest for late filing, default in advance tax and deferment of advance tax (sections 234A, 234B and 234C of the 1961 Act) is 1% per month or part of a month. Use the Calculators tab for exact figures.
